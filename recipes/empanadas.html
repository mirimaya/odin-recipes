Description

This beef empanada recipe is made with Goya Discos pastry rounds and a mouthwatering filling of seasoned ground beef, tomatoes, onions, and garlic. Enjoy these flaky, deep-fried empanadas as an appetizer, a main dish, or even after your main course.

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon of Goya extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 pound of ground beef
  • 1/2 medium-sized yellow onion, finely chopped
  • 1/4 cup of Goya tomato sauce
  • 6 Goya spanish olives stuffed with minced pimientos, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons of Goya sofrito
  • 1 packet Goya Sazon with coriander and annatto
  • 1 teaspoon of Goya minced garlic
  • 1 teaspoon of Goya dried oregano
  • 1 (14oz) package of yellow or white Goya Discos empanada discs, thawed
  • 1 quart of Goya corn oil, for frying

Steps

  1. Gather the ingredients.
  2. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add ground beef; cook and stir until browned and crumbly (about 5 minutes).
  3. Stir in tomato sauce, olives, sofrito, sazón, garlic, oregano, and pepper. Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer until mixture thickens (about 15 minutes).
  4. Roll empanada disks on a lightly floured surface until 1/2 inch larger in diameter. Spoon about 1 tablespook of meat mixture into the middle of each disk. Moisten the disk edges with water, fold in half over filling to form a half-moon, and pinch to seal. (or seal with a fork)
  5. Head 2 1/2 inches of corn oil in a deep-fryer or large saucepan to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Working batches, fry empanadas until crisp and golden brown, flipping once, 4 to 6 minutes.
  6. Transfer to paper towels to drain. Enjoy!
