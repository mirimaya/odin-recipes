Description

This teriyaki chicken recipe uses a homemade teriyaki sauce to glaze chicken thighs that are baked in the oven for the most delicious sticky chicken. This is a much-requested recipe in our house! Easy to double for a large group.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup of white sugar
  • 1/2 cup of soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup of apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon of cornstarch
  • 1 clove of garlic, minced
  • 1/2 teaspoon of ground ginger
  • 1/4 teaspoon of ground black pepper
  • 12 boneless, skinless chicken thighs

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 425 degrees F (220 degrees C). Lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  2. Combine sugar, soy sauce, vinegar, cornstarch, cold water, garlic, ginger, and black pepper in a small saucepan over low heat; simmer, stirring frequently, until teriyaki sauce thickens and bubbles (3 to 5 minutes). Remove from the heat.
  3. Place chicken thighs in the prepared baking dish; brush both sides of each thigh with teriyaki sauce. Reserve any extra sauce for basting.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven for 30 minutes.
  5. Flip chicken; brush with sauce. Continue baking, basting with remaining sauce every 10 minutes, until no longer pink and juices run clear, (20 to 30 minutes more).
  6. Serve hot and enjoy!
