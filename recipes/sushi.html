Description

Sushi rolls that can be filled with any ingredients you choose. Try smoked salmon instead of imitation crabmeat. Serve with teriyaki sauce and wasabi.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/3 cups of water
  • 2/3 cup of uncooked short-grain white rice
  • 3 tablespoons of rice vinegar
  • 3 tablespoons of white sugar
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons of salt
  • 4 sheets of nori seaweed
  • 1/2 pound of imitation crabmeat, flaked
  • 1 avocado peeled, pitted, and sliced
  • 1/2 cucumber, peeled, pitted, and sliced
  • 2 tablespoons of picked ginger

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 300 degrees F. (150 degrees C).
  2. Bring water to a boil in a medium pot; stir in rice. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer until rice is tender and water has been absorbed (about 20-25 minutes).
  3. Mix rice vinegar, sugar, and salt in a small bowl. Gently stir into cooked rice in the pot and set aside, uncovered, to let the rice cool.
  4. Lay nori sheeds on a baking sheet.
  5. Heat nori in the preheated oven until warm (about 1 to 2 minutes)
  6. Center 1 nori sheet on a bamboo sushi mat. Use wet hands to spread a thin layer of rice on top. Arrange 1/4 of the crabmeat, avocado, cucumber, and pickled ginger over rice in a line down the center.
  7. Lift one end of the mat and roll it tightly over filling to make a complete roll. Repeat with remaining ingredients.
  8. Use a wet, sharp knife to cut each roll into 4 to 6 slices. Enjoy!
